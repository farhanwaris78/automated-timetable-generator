    How to use

    1. Add your data - teachers (Alt+T), classrooms (Alt+R) and courses with their codes (Alt+C). Everything is editable under Manage data (Alt+M).
    2. Pick a shift - Morning (Alt+1) or Evening (Alt+2). Each shift keeps its own hours; both are saved in the same timetable.
    3. Generate the grid for up to 7 days a week.
    4. Drag & drop courses into room/time cells. Every drop is checked for room, teacher, student and capacity clashes.
    5. Undo/redo anything with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.
    6. Save (Ctrl+S) and export to Excel (Ctrl+E) - one worksheet per day, plus Summary and By-Teacher sheets.
    7. Labs & semesters - tick This course has a lab in the course editor and the lab gets its own draggable card; give the course a semester and the app blocks two classes of the same semester-section at the same time. Excel export writes one sheet per semester.
    8. Publish (Ctrl+Shift+P) - per-teacher, per-section, per-semester or per-room PDFs, and a live calendar feed students can subscribe to.
    9. Import (Ctrl+I) - bulk-load teachers, rooms and courses from a spreadsheet instead of typing them one by one.
